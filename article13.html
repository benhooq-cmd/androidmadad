Title: Android Me Hidden Apps Kaise Dekhain? Complete SEO Guide (Step-by-Step, 2026) Kayi dafa Android phone me kuch apps hidden (chhupi hui) hoti hain jo home screen ya app drawer me nazar nahi aati. Ye apps ya to launcher settings ki wajah se hide hoti hain, ya parental control, ya phir system level par disabled hoti hain. Kuch cases me users khud privacy ke liye apps hide kar dete hain aur baad me bhool jate hain. Is detailed SEO guide me aap seekhenge Android me hidden apps dekhne ke tamam tareeqe — bina kisi third-party tool ke. Ye methods Samsung, Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, Realme, Infinix, Tecno aur stock Android sab par kaam karte hain. 🔎 Hidden Apps Kya Hoti Hain? Hidden apps wo applications hoti hain jo: App drawer me show nahi hoti Home screen par nazar nahi aati Launcher settings se hide ki gayi hoti hain Parental control ya app lock me chhupi hoti hain System apps ki list me disabled hoti hain ✅ Method 1: App Drawer Settings Se Hidden Apps Dekhna Aksar launchers me Hide Apps ka option hota hai. Steps: Home screen par long press karein Settings / Home Settings open karein Hide Apps ya Hidden Apps option par tap karein Yahan se sari hidden apps ki list mil jati hai Samsung, Xiaomi (MIUI), Realme UI, Oppo ColorOS me ye option hota hai. ✅ Method 2: Phone Settings → Apps List Se Har app chahe hidden ho, Apps list me zaroor hoti hai. Steps: Settings open karein Apps / Manage Apps par jayein All Apps select karein List scroll karein — yahan hidden apps bhi mil jati hain Yahan se aap app ko open, enable, ya uninstall bhi kar sakte hain. ✅ Method 3: Search Bar Se App Find Karein App drawer ke upar search bar hoti hai. App ka naam type karein Agar app hidden hai lekin installed hai, to search me show ho jati hai ✅ Method 4: Play Store Se Check Karein Play Store batata hai kaunsi apps installed hain. Steps: Play Store open karein Profile icon → Manage apps & device Manage tab me Installed dekhein Yahan hidden apps bhi list me hoti hain. ✅ Method 5: Special Access / App Permissions Check Karein Kuch spy ya hidden apps Special App Access me milti hain. Steps: Settings → Privacy → Special App Access → Device admin apps Appear on top Usage access Yahan suspicious apps mil sakti hain. ✅ Method 6: Disabled Apps Ko Enable Karein Kuch apps disable hoti hain is liye nazar nahi aati. Steps: Settings → Apps → Filter: Disabled Apps Agar koi important app disabled ho to Enable karein. ✅ Method 7: Parental Control / App Lock Check Karein Agar phone me App Lock ya Parental Control on hai: App Lock settings open karein Hidden/Locked apps ki list dekhein ✅ Method 8: Launcher Change Karke Dekhein Kabhi kabhi default launcher apps hide karta hai. Play Store se Nova Launcher install karein Nova Launcher me app drawer open karein Hidden apps nazar aa jati hain ✅ Method 9: Safe Mode Me Check Karein Safe Mode me sirf system apps chalti hain. Agar Safe Mode me koi unknown app na ho aur normal mode me ho, to wo third-party hidden app ho sakti hai. 🎯 Common Reasons Apps Hidden Kyu Hoti Hain?
