Lekin in dono apps ka ek bada drawback ye hai ke ye bohot zyada mobile data consume karti hain. Videos, reels, stories, auto-play aur background refresh ki wajah se data bohot jaldi khatam ho jata hai. Agar aap bhi pareshaan hain ke Facebook aur Instagram data kam kaise karein, to ye article aapke liye complete solution hai. Yahan hum step-by-step secret settings aur smart tips share karenge jo aapka data kaafi had tak save kar sakti hain. Facebook Aur Instagram Itna Zyada Data Kyun Use Karte Hain? In apps ka data zyada kharch hone ki main wajahen: · Auto-play videos aur reels · High quality images & videos · Background refresh · Ads aur sponsored content · Live videos aur stories Agar in cheezon ko control na kiya jaye, to daily data limit bohot jaldi cross ho jati hai. 1. Facebook Mein Data Saver Mode Enable Karein Facebook ka Data Saver Mode bohot effective hai, lekin zyada log isay enable nahi karte. Facebook Data Saver Enable Karne Ka Tareeqa: · Facebook app open karein · Menu (☰) par tap karein · Settings & Privacy > Settings · Media > Data Saver ON Fayda: · Videos auto-play nahi hoti · Low quality media load hoti hai · Data consumption kaafi kam ho jata hai 2. Instagram Mein Data Saver Mode Enable Karein Instagram bhi ab built-in Data Saver option deta hai. Instagram Data Saver Enable Karne Ka Tareeqa: 1.Instagram open karein 2.Profile > Menu (☰) 3.Settings > Data Usage 4.Data Saver ON karein 📌 Is se videos dheere load hoti hain, lekin data save hota hai. 3. Auto-Play Videos Band Karein (Most Important 🔥) Auto-play videos Facebook aur Instagram dono par sabse zyada data waste karti hain. Facebook: · Settings > Media · Auto-play videos → Never Instagram: · Data Saver ON karne ke baad auto-play limit ho jata hai 4. Facebook Lite Aur Instagram Lite Use Karein Heavy apps ki jagah Lite versions use karna data save karne ka best tareeqa hai. Lite Apps Ke Fayde: · 50–70% kam data use · Fast loading · Low RAM aur battery usage Best Lite Apps: · Facebook Lite · Instagram Lite 5. Reels Aur Stories Kam Dekhein Instagram Reels aur Facebook Stories continuous auto-play ki wajah se bohot zyada data consume karti hain. Smart Tip: · Sirf zaroori reels dekhein · Endless scrolling se bachein · Wi-Fi par reels enjoy karein 6. Image & Video Quality Kam Rakhein High resolution media sabse zyada data kharch karta hai. Facebook Settings: · Media > Upload Photos in HD → OFF · Upload Videos in HD → OFF Instagram Settings: · Data Usage > High-quality uploads → OFF 7. Background Data Restrict Karein (Android Users) Apps background mein data use karti rehti hain, jo aapko pata bhi nahi chalta. Kaise? · Settings > Apps · Facebook / Instagram select karein · Mobile Data & Wi-Fi · Background Data OFF 8. Browser Se Facebook & Instagram Use Karein Agar data bohot limited hai to apps ke bajaye browser use karein. Best Browsers: · Opera Mini · Google Chrome (Lite Mode ON) Opera Mini data compression use karta hai, jo kaafi data save karta hai. 9. Notifications Aur Sync Kam Karein Notifications ke sath images aur previews bhi load hoti hain. Solution: · Facebook & Instagram settings mein · Unnecessary notifications OFF karein 10.
