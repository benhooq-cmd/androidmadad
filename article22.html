Android me traditional PC jaisa virus kam hota hai, lekin **harmful apps** zyada common hoti hain jo background me chal kar problem create karti hain. Is complete SEO guide me aap seekhenge **Android phone se virus kaise remove karein**, kaise identify karein, aur future me kaise bachain — step by step, practical tareeqon ke sath. --- ## 🔍 Android Virus / Malware Kya Hota Hai? Android me harmful cheezein aksar in forms me hoti hain: * **Adware:** Har jagah pop-up ads * **Spyware:** Data chori karta hai * **Fake Apps:** Real app jaisi lagti hain * **Background Malware:** RAM, battery, data use karta hai * **Browser Hijacker:** Browser homepage change --- ## ⚠️ Virus Hone Ki Common Nishaniyan | Sign | Detail | | ----------------- | -------------------------- | | Phone slow / hang | Performance down | | Pop-up ads | Home screen par ads | | Unknown apps | Khud install ho jati | | Data drain | Internet jaldi khatam | | Battery heat | Phone garam | | Settings change | Browser ya settings change | Agar in me se 2–3 signs mil rahe hain to action lena zaroori hai. --- ## ✅ Step 1: Safe Mode Me Phone Start Karein (Sab Se Important) Safe Mode me sirf system apps chalti hain. Yahan se pata chalta hai ke problem third-party app me hai. **Safe Mode ON:** * Power button hold karein * Power Off option ko hold karein * Safe Mode select karein Agar Safe Mode me ads/issue band ho jaye to koi installed app culprit hai. --- ## ✅ Step 2: Suspicious Apps Uninstall Karein Settings → Apps → All Apps Check karein: * Unknown naam * Without icon * Recently installed Un apps ko **Uninstall** karein. --- ## ✅ Step 3: Google Play Protect Se Scan Karein Play Store → Profile → Play Protect → Scan Ye harmful apps detect karta hai. --- ## ✅ Step 4: Antivirus App Se Full Scan Karein Trusted antivirus install karein: * Avast * Kaspersky * Bitdefender Full device scan karein aur threats remove karein. --- ## ✅ Step 5: App Permissions Check Karein Settings → Privacy → Permission Manager Camera, mic, location ki permissions suspicious apps se remove karein. --- ## ✅ Step 6: Browser Reset Karein Agar ads browser me aa rahi hain: * Browser settings reset * Cache clear * Unknown extensions remove --- ## ✅ Step 7: Unknown Sources OFF Karein Settings → Security → Install Unknown Apps → OFF Ye future virus ko rokta hai. --- ## ✅ Step 8: Downloaded Files Check Karein File Manager → Downloads Unknown APK files delete karein. --- ## ✅ Step 9: System Update Karein Settings → Software Update Security patches malware remove karte hain. --- ## ✅ Step 10: Factory Reset (Last & 100% Working) Agar virus remove na ho: 1.
