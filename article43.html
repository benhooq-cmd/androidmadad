🔁 Step 2: Accounts Ke Darmiyan Switch Karna Gmail / Play Store / YouTube open karein → Profile icon → Account select --- ## ⭐ Step 3: Default Google Account Set Karna (Play Store) Play Store → Profile → Account → jis se apps install karni hon us par switch > Note: Play Store default pehla added account hota hai. --- ## 🔄 Step 4: Sync Settings Har Account Ke Liye Alag Settings → Accounts → Google → Account select → Sync options Aap decide kar sakte hain: * Contacts * Drive * Gmail * Calendar --- ## 📇 Step 5: Contacts Mix Hone Se Bachain Contacts app → Filter / Customize view → Specific account select --- ## ☁️ Step 6: Google Drive & Photos Backup Control Photos → Profile → Backup → Account choose Drive → Settings → Account switch --- ## 🔔 Step 7: Notifications Manage Karein Har Gmail account ki notifications alag set karein. --- ##
