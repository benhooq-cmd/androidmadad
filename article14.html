Title: Android Phone Me Duplicate Apps Delete Kaise Karein? Complete SEO Guide (2026) Android phone use karte waqt aksar users ko ek common problem face hoti hai: duplicate apps. Kabhi ek hi app do dafa nazar aati hai, kabhi same naam ki do apps hoti hain, ya kabhi system app aur Play Store app dono alag alag dikhte hain. Is se na sirf phone ki storage waste hoti hai, balki performance bhi slow ho jata hai. Is detailed SEO guide me aap seekhenge Android me duplicate apps ko identify aur delete karne ke tamam working tareeqe — Samsung, Xiaomi, Realme, Oppo, Vivo, Infinix, Tecno aur stock Android sab par. 🔍 Duplicate Apps Kya Hoti Hain? Steps: Duplicate app icon par long press Uninstall par tap karein Agar uninstall option na aaye to next methods follow karein. ✅ Method 2: Settings → Apps Se Delete Karein Yahan se har app ka control milta hai. Steps: Settings open karein Apps / Manage Apps List me duplicate app find karein Uninstall ya Disable karein ✅ Method 3: Dual Apps / Clone Apps Band Karein Aksar phones me Dual Apps feature hota hai. Path (brands ke mutabiq): Xiaomi: Settings → Apps → Dual Apps Samsung: Settings → Advanced Features → Dual Messenger Realme/Oppo/Vivo: App Clone Yahan se cloned app ko Remove karein. ✅ Method 4: Work Profile Check Karein Agar phone me work profile bana ho to apps double hoti hain. Steps: Settings → Passwords & Accounts → Work Profile Work profile delete karne se duplicate apps hat jati hain. ✅ Method 5: Launcher Issue Fix Karein Kabhi kabhi sirf icon duplicate hota hai, app nahi. Fix: Launcher cache clear karein Phone restart karein Settings → Apps → Launcher → Storage → Clear Cache ✅ Method 6: System Apps Ko Disable Karein Kuch system apps uninstall nahi hoti, lekin disable ho sakti hain. Steps: Settings → Apps → System App → Disable ✅ Method 7: Play Store Se Check Karein Play Store batata hai kaunsi apps actually installed hain. Play Store → Manage Apps → Installed Yahan se extra app uninstall karein. ✅ Method 8: Safe Mode Me Check Karein Safe Mode me sirf original apps hoti hain. Agar duplicate Safe Mode me na ho to wo third-party issue hai. ✅ Method 9: Third-Party Cloning Apps Remove Karein Parallel Space, Dual Space, Multiple Accounts jaisi apps duplicates banati hain. In apps ko uninstall karein. ✅ Method 10: Factory Reset (Last Option) Agar problem solve na ho to backup le kar factory reset karein. 🧠 Pro Tips Dual Apps feature bina zarurat on na rakhein Cloning apps avoid karein Regular apps list check karein System apps ko uninstall na karein, sirf disable karein ❓ FAQs – Duplicate Apps Delete Karne Ke Bare Me Q1: Ek hi app do bar kyu nazar aati hai?
