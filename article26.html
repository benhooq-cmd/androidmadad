✅ Step 3: WhatsApp Storage Manage Karein WhatsApp → Settings → Storage and Data → Manage Storage Large files delete karein. --- ## ✅ Step 4: Duplicate Photos/Videos Delete Karein Google Files app → Clean → Duplicate files --- ## ✅ Step 5: Unused Apps Uninstall Karein Settings → Apps → Uninstall unused apps --- ## ✅ Step 6: Downloads Folder Clean Karein File Manager → Downloads → Delete unnecessary files --- ## ✅ Step 7: Gallery Ka Trash Folder Empty Karein Gallery → Trash/Recycle Bin → Empty --- ## ✅ Step 8: Cloud Backup Use Karein Google Photos me backup ON karke device se photos delete karein. --- ## ✅ Step 9: Offline Videos Delete Karein YouTube/Netflix downloads remove karein. --- ## ✅ Step 10: Lite Apps Use Karein Facebook Lite, Messenger Lite, YouTube Lite kam space lete hain. --- ##
