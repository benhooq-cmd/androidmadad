**Title: Android Phone Me Virus Kaise Check Karein? Complete SEO Guide (2026)** Agar aapka Android phone achanak slow ho gaya hai, ads bar-bar aa rahi hain, unknown apps nazar aa rahi hain, ya data jaldi khatam ho raha hai — to mumkin hai ke phone me **virus, malware, ya harmful app** ho. Android me traditional “virus” kam hota hai, lekin **malicious apps, adware, spyware** zyada common hote hain. Is complete SEO guide me aap seekhenge ke **Android phone me virus kaise check karein**, kaise remove karein, aur future me kaise bachain — step by step. --- ## 🔍 Android Virus / Malware Kya Hota Hai? Android me harmful cheezein aksar in forms me hoti hain: * Adware (bar-bar ads) * Spyware (data chori) * Fake apps * Background me chalne wali suspicious apps * Browser hijacker --- ## ⚠️ Virus Hone Ki Common Signs | Sign | Detail | | --------------- | ----------------------- | | Phone slow | Apps hang / lag | | Pop-up ads | Home screen par ads | | Unknown apps | Khud se install | | Data drain | Internet jaldi khatam | | Battery drain | Phone garam rehta | | Settings change | Browser homepage change | --- ## ✅ Step 1: Google Play Protect Se Virus Scan Karein Play Protect Android ka built-in antivirus hai. **Steps:** 1. Play Store open karein 2. Profile icon → Play Protect 3. **Scan** par tap karein Agar harmful app hogi to detect ho jaye gi. --- ## ✅ Step 2: Unknown Apps Check Karein Settings → Apps → All Apps List me dekhein: * Unknown naam wali apps * Without icon apps * Recently installed suspicious apps Uninstall karein. --- ## ✅ Step 3: Safe Mode Me Phone Start Karein Safe Mode me sirf system apps chalti hain. **Safe Mode kaise on karein:** * Power button hold → Power off hold → Safe Mode Agar ads/issue Safe Mode me band ho jaye to problem third-party app me hai. --- ## ✅ Step 4: App Permissions Check Karein Spy apps unnecessary permissions leti hain. Settings → Privacy → Permission Manager Check karein: * Camera * Microphone * Location * Storage Suspicious app se permissions remove karein. --- ## ✅ Step 5: Browser Check Karein Agar browser me ads aa rahi hain: * Browser settings reset karein * Unknown extensions remove karein * Cache clear karein --- ## ✅ Step 6: Antivirus App Se Scan Karein Trusted antivirus: * Avast * Bitdefender * Kaspersky Play Store se install karke full scan karein. --- ## ✅ Step 7: Unknown Sources Off Karein Settings → Security → Install Unknown Apps → OFF --- ## ✅ Step 8: Recently Installed Apps Remove Karein Jo apps last days me install ki ho aur issue uske baad start hua ho, uninstall karein. --- ## ✅ Step 9: System Update Karein Security patches virus remove karte hain. Settings → Software Update --- ## ✅ Step 10: Factory Reset (Last Option) Agar virus remove na ho: * Backup lein * Factory reset karein --- ## 🧠 Future Me Virus Se Bachne Ke Tips * Sirf Play Store se apps install karein * Mod apps avoid karein * Random links par click na karein * Regular Play Protect scan karein --- ## ❓ FAQs – Android Phone Me Virus Check **Q1: Android me virus kaise aata hai?** Unknown apps aur mod APK se. **Q2: Play Protect kafi hai?** Basic protection ke liye haan. **Q3: Safe Mode ka kya faida hai?** Malicious app identify hoti hai. **Q4: Ads kyu aati hain?** Adware app ki wajah se. **Q5: Antivirus zaroori hai?** Agar issue ho to haan. **Q6: Permissions check karna kyu?** Spy apps data access karti hain. **Q7: Factory reset se virus hat jata hai?** Haan, 100%. **Q8: Unknown sources kya hota hai?** Play Store ke ilawa app install option. **Q9: Phone slow hona virus ki nishani hai?** Ho sakta hai. **Q10: System update kyu zaroori hai?** Security bugs fix hote hain. --- In steps ko follow karke aap asaani se **Android phone me virus check aur remove** kar sakte hain aur apne device ko secure rakh sakte hain.
