Jo status photo/video chahiye, usay **Copy** karein 7. Apne gallery folder me **Paste** karein Ab wo status permanently aapke phone me save ho gaya. > Agar .Statuses folder nazar na aaye to “Show Hidden Files” ON karein. --- ## ✅ Method 2: Status Saver Apps (Agar File Manager Mushkil Lage) Play Store par “Status Saver for WhatsApp” apps mil jati hain. **Steps:** 1. Download button se save karein > Sirf trusted apps use karein. --- ## ✅ Method 3: Screenshot / Screen Recording Agar upar wale methods use na karna chahen: * Photo status ka screenshot lein * Video status ki screen recording karein Phir gallery se crop/edit karein. --- ## ⚠️ Important: Pehle Status Dekhna Zaroori Hai .Statuses folder me sirf wahi status aata hai **jo aap dekh chuke ho**.
